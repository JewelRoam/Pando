# experiments/ - archived research harnesses

Moved here on 2026-10-17 by a swarm decision: the win-root `/decide` vote
on "archive vs delete vs index" chose **archive** (2/3 members, tier
`verify` - the gate asked for human confirmation, and archiving is the
reversible reading of that mandate).

These are historical experiment scripts. They work, they produced numbers
quoted in `docs/archive/research/*` and `docs/evidence/reflections.md`, and they are NOT
part of the resident system: CI does not compile them and `pando/` does
not import them.

Policy: things land here when they stop being operational but their
numbers are still cited. Delete only after the citing docs no longer need
them. `tools/README.md` lists what remains in `tools/` and why.
