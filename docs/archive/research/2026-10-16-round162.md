## What the strength axis shows

**Precision is the invariant.** Every configuration on both splits keeps
auto accuracy at exactly 1.0000 - the assent rule (the trusted member must
back the majority, with a declared fallback for the demoted rows) is what
makes weak tiers safe to seat, and it holds across tier count, tier
strength, and wording inventory.

**A stronger weak-tier buys coverage, not decided accuracy.** The 8B
automates 233 rows on E with every one correct (97% coverage; core: 199;
4B: 227) and 208 on D (core: 159). Decided accuracy is set by the demoted
rows, whose answer comes from the fallback policy - so the tier adds
coverage while the policy owns quality.

**Stacking weak tiers dilutes again.** 4B+8B together automate *fewer* rows
than the 8B alone (221 vs 233 on E; 186 vs 208 on D): an extra majority
source reshapes which rows lose the trusted member's backing. The round-155
exchange rate generalizes: every added tier must justify itself, and tiers
below the trusted member buy coverage only.

**The fallback flip reconfirmed:** veto wins E (0.9917), advisor wins D
(0.9750), now across 4B, 8B, and both.
