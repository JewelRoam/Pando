# Research Round 121 — The quorum should not care how many copies you keep

Date: 2026-10-15

## From a measurement to a rule

Round 119 measured what a replica does to a quorum: adding a second `english`
dropped the weighted vote from 90.4% to 85.4%, which is exactly `english`'s
own accuracy, because a 2:1 majority of (english, english-copy,
typed-decisions) *is* english. The swarm's whole growth story is copying a
lineage onto more devices, so that fact has to become a system property
rather than a documented hazard.

Three changes, all small:

- `pandosi/common.py` gains `collapse_lineages()`, which reduces same-lineage
  members to one vote (the choice carrying the most probability mass, at the
  mean probability of the members backing it), and `aggregate()` gains
  `count_lineages=True`, which collapses before counting - so `min_quorum`
  applies to lineages too.
- `pandosi/rootd.py` gains `Coordinator.count_lineages` and the
  `rootd --lineage-quorum` flag. Default off: turning it on changes which
  decisions auto-execute, which is an operator's call.
- `tools/lineage_quorum_check.py` replays a recorded report through both
  policies with replicas added, so the property is measured rather than
  asserted. No models are re-run: the votes are held fixed, so any difference
  between the columns is caused by the counting rule alone.

## The property: invariance under replication

240 recorded rows from the round-118 trio, replicas of each lineage added in
turn:

| scenario | stems | lineages | shipped full | collapse full | shipped auto | collapse auto |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| no replica | 3 | 3 | 0.892 | 0.892 | 0.817 | 0.817 |
| +1 english | 4 | 3 | 0.842 | **0.892** | 0.783 | **0.817** |
| +2 english | 5 | 3 | 0.854 | **0.892** | 0.825 | **0.817** |
| +1 multilingual | 4 | 3 | 0.746 | **0.892** | 0.717 | **0.817** |
| +2 multilingual | 5 | 3 | 0.758 | **0.892** | 0.800 | **0.817** |
| +1 typed-decisions | 4 | 3 | 0.883 | **0.892** | 0.771 | **0.817** |
| +2 typed-decisions | 5 | 3 | 0.900 | **0.892** | 0.700 | **0.817** |

Replication moves the shipped answer by **up to 14.6 points** of full-coverage
accuracy and by up to 11.7 points of auto-accepted coverage, depending only
on which lineage was copied. It moves the lineage-counted answer by **0.000**,
in every scenario - and the no-replica row shows the two policies identical,
because collapsing a group of one is the identity. A diverse quorum loses
nothing; a replicated one stops being able to lie to itself.

## What the drift was measuring

The shipped rule lets a *copy count as evidence*, so its accuracy is a
function of the copy pattern rather than of the swarm:

- replicate the weakest member (`multilingual`, 75.0%) and the quorum falls
  toward 74.6% - the swarm converges on its worst voter;
- replicate the best member (`typed-decisions`, 88.5%) and the quorum *rises*
  to 90.0%. That is the trap, not the fix: it rewards an operator for knowing
  which lineage is best, which rounds 117 and 118 measured to be exactly the
  ordering that is noisy at small n, and it means accuracy can be bought by
  adding hardware rather than by adding information.

Under the lineage rule both cases collapse to the same number, which is the
correct answer for a vote that carries no new information.

## What this does not fix

Near-duplicates registered under **different checkpoint names**. Round 119's
`rlcd-v7` is such a case - 3 names, 2 effective deciders - and collapsing by
name cannot see it:

| scenario (trio B: english, rlcd-v7, typed-decisions) | shipped full | collapse full |
|---|---:|---:|
| no replica | 0.854 | 0.854 |
| +1 rlcd-v7 | 0.854 | 0.854 |
| +2 rlcd-v7 | 0.854 | 0.854 |
| +2 typed-decisions | 0.900 | 0.854 |

The collapsed column is honest here rather than better: it refuses to let
"replicate the strong member" manufacture accuracy out of a quorum that has
only two opinions in it. What the collapsed column cannot do is recover the
second opinion, because the second opinion was never there. That fix belongs
at admission time - a candidate that never disagrees with an admitted lineage
should be refused or discounted, and round 119 produced the number for one
candidate (0 disagreements in 240 tasks, phi +1.000).

## Status

| decision | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| which lineage a replica votes as | its own full-weight vote | folded into its lineage |
| accuracy under replication | drifts up to 14.6 pt by copy pattern | invariant |
| diverse quorum (3 distinct lineages) | 0.892 / 0.817 | unchanged |
| near-duplicate under a new name | admitted at full weight | still admitted - open |
