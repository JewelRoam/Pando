  (seq=128 + seq=256, markers=8, both surgery-applied); run() picks the
  smallest bucket that fits, pads, and delegates. One compile per bucket at
  load; zero recompiles at serve time.

  (greeting@0.88, 30-38 ms); long input -> 256 bucket
  (refund@0.80, 31-40 ms); bucket reuse confirmed (no stalls).

  40-task suite class of workloads at 25-40 ms without per-shape compiles.
