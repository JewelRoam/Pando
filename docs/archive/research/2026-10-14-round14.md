Three axes of heterogeneity in one quorum: backend (OpenVINO vs onnxruntime),
device (iGPU vs CPU), checkpoint (en vs ml lineage).

## Engineering added

- `OnnxLayaBackend(device=...)`: when device != CPU, the ONNXAgent's ORT
  session is replaced by an OpenVINO lazy-session shim.
- Lazy per-shape compilation: the Laya graph's dynamic dims cannot be
  pre-shaped (marker counts vary per question), so OV models are compiled on
  first sight of a concrete input-shape tuple and cached. Warmup decide runs
  at stem load so compilation never lands inside a vote.
- rootd CALL_TIMEOUT raised 5s -> 30s (GPU compile latency).
- Bugs fixed en route: missing numpy import in the shim; warmup-before-vote.

## Notes

- gpu-en probabilities differ from cpu-ml (0.9883 vs 0.9875) - backend
  numerics add a small natural diversity on top of checkpoint diversity.
- Lazy compile cache key = exact input-shape tuple; new question shapes cost
  one recompile (~8 s GPU) - acceptable for decision workloads, documented.
