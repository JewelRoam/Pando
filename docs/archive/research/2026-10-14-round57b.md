# Research Round 57b — split-case majority reliability: 36% (2026-10-14)

## THE KEY FINDING

When the 3 lineages disagree (14/40 = 35% of tasks), the majority vote is
only 36% reliable (5/14 match expected). This PROVES:

1. **Auto-executing split decisions would be dangerous** - 64% error rate
2. **The verify/escalate routing is NECESSARY, not optional** - it catches
   exactly the cases where the swarm is unreliable
3. **These 14 cases are the RLCD training fuel** - teaching the lineages
   to agree on these (with correct labels) would reduce the split rate
   and increase overall swarm reliability

## The system is working as designed

- Unanimous cases (65%): high confidence, auto-execute ✓
- Split cases (35%): low reliability, route to verify ✓
- The 0.6 auto_min_agreement threshold correctly separates the two

## Phase 5 implication

14 contrast pairs × per-lineage votes = sufficient data for a first RLCD
fine-tune round. Target: reduce split rate from 35% to <20%.
