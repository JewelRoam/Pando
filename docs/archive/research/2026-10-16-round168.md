# Research Round 168 — The loop's first full turn: a teacher, a student, and an island

Date: 2026-10-16

Two Mac tasks completed (020 corpus, 022 island) and the Windows side ran
the fine-tune the corpus was for. Three results, one of them deflationary.

## The corpus (Task 020)

Set G: 1040 rows, 48 new cues, zero cue overlap with A/A2/B/C/D/E/F. The
8B and 4B teachers labeled it independently; **918 rows survived the
dual-teacher filter (88.3% agreement)**; the 122 disagreements are recorded
as the hard cases. Tools (`tools/distill_corpus.py`, `build_large_suite.py
--cues g`) and the corpus are in the repo.

## ft-v5: the student did what it was asked, and that is not enough

Split: every 10th row held out (91 rows), 827 trained (unfrozen, 1 epoch,
seed 5). Teacher-agreement on the held-out wording of the same generation:

| member | teacher-agreement (holdout, 91 rows) | D | E | F |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| english (floor) | 0.8352 | 0.7292 | 0.8375 | 0.8417 |
| ft-v3 (incumbent) | 0.9451 | 0.9417 | 0.9917 | 0.8667 |
| **ft-v5 (distilled)** | **1.0000** | 0.9208 | 0.8708 | 0.8500 |

**ft-v5 matches its teacher perfectly on the teacher's own distribution**
(1.0000; caveat: train/holdout share the 48 cues, so this measures
fidelity to the generation, not generalization) and beats the floor tier
everywhere. But it loses to ft-v3 on every other evaluator - decisively on
E (0.8708 vs 0.9917). Under round 153's admission rule (capability on
unseen wording, fleet must not regress) **ft-v5 is refused**: the
distillation fit this generation's phrasing hard and paid for it across
distributions. It also arrived with healthy, un-saturated confidences
(0.13-0.998) - the one property it strictly improves.

The reading: the loop turns mechanically (teacher -> labels -> student ->
votes, all measured), but a student is not a replacement for its teacher
outside the teacher's distribution, and a *different-split* incumbent
still holds the seat. The next turn has an obvious knob: mix corpora
(distill G with the 720 human-labeled rows) and/or replicate seeds; the
admission machinery already knows how to judge it.

## The island (Task 022)

The Mac root now serves two workers - `mac-text` (:8766) and
`mac-llm-8b-local` (:8768, launchd KeepAlive, ~1.6 s EWMA) - with island
`quorum_k=1`, so the bridge to Windows exposes the island's own rotation
between its floor tier and its LLM tier. Round 157's "a far group is one
vote" now has a two-tier instance; the endpoint branch records the
membership. Windows-side live seats still use the LLM directly, so no
existing number changes.
