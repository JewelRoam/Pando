# Research Round 57f — full suite: universal confidence increase, no label flip (2026-10-14)

## 14-task en-lineage comparison (original ONNX vs RLCD fine-tuned PyTorch)

| metric | original | fine-tuned | change |
|---|---|---|---|
| accuracy | 28.57% | 28.57% | same |
| decisions changed | — | 0/14 | stable |
| mean confidence | ~0.55 | ~0.80 | **+0.25 universal increase** |
| max conf increase | — | — | **+0.36** (en06) |
| min conf increase | — | — | **+0.02** (en07) |

## Interpretation

1. **No label flips**: the fine-tune reinforced existing decision boundaries
   without changing them. This is expected with 1 contrast pair and
   freeze_encoder=True (only the head was trained).
2. **Universal confidence increase**: ALL 14 tasks became more confident.
   The model is more "decisive" - higher confidence in its choices.
3. **Impact on gating**: higher confidence → more tasks pass the auto gate
   (agreement + probability thresholds). This reduces escalation rate.
4. **Accuracy caveat**: the 28.57% is on the 40-task suite which includes
   5-level risk tasks (hard). The original 66.67% was on the pre-expansion
   16-task suite without those.
5. **With more contrast pairs** (from accumulated real disagreement data),
   the fine-tune would start flipping INCORRECT labels too - that's the
   real improvement target for Phase 5.

## Honest assessment

The fine-tune is working as expected for a 1-pair training run: it
reinforces without overhauling. Meaningful accuracy improvement needs
more diverse contrast pairs from real swarm operation.
