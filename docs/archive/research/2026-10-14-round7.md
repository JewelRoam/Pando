## Product adoption design landed (docs/design/ADOPTION.md)

- Three-ring capability model: Ring 0 zero-privilege join (pip + join + mDNS,
  valuable on ONE machine via intra-device EU consensus); Ring 1 per-action
  consent with `pandosi doctor` capability reports and signed capability
  tokens (reuse Spore manifest format); Ring 2 sandbox-gated lab (Docker/
  podman/WSL2 required, Spore in a separate optional package).
- Survival metrics: clone-to-first-vote <5 min / <3 commands; every failure
  prints the fix; nothing may break Ring 0 cleanliness.
- Platform honesty: per-OS silent installers are out of scope; detect,
  explain, then ask.
