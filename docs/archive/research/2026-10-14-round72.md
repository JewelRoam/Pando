# Research Round 72 — Hierarchical two-group consensus

Date: 2026-10-14

## Architecture

Two independent PandoSI groups now communicate hierarchically:

```text
Group A root
  +-- three local stems
  +-- bridge agent
        -> Group B root
             +-- three local stems
```

`pandosi.bridge` reuses the normal stem HTTP contract. From Group A's point
of view, Group B's aggregate decision is simply one extra checkpoint named
`group-b`.

This is the agent-parallel topology for groups that may be separated by a
slower network: each group keeps a local quorum and only its summary vote
crosses the group boundary.

## Joined result

With both groups healthy:

```text
responded:       4
quorum_size:     4
fan-out:         176.9 ms
local checkpoints: a-en, a-ml, a-td
bridge checkpoint: group-b
```

The parent root therefore combined three local decisions and one remote
group aggregate without knowing anything about Group B's internal workers.

## Partition result

Group B's root was terminated while its bridge process remained registered
with Group A.

First decision after partition:

```text
responded:       3
quorum_size:     4
fan-out:         959 ms
checkpoints:     a-en, a-ml, a-td
```

The bridge failed while the three local workers continued to answer.

Next immediate decision:

```text
responded:       3
quorum_size:     3
fan-out:         13.2 ms
checkpoints:     a-en, a-ml, a-td
```

The failed bridge entered cooldown and was removed from selection. The
local group continued without waiting for the partitioned remote group.

## Interpretation

This validates the first concrete version of the two-group design:

- group-local consensus remains independent;
- one bridge vote represents an entire remote group;
- checkpoint diversity still applies at the parent level;
- loss of the remote group degrades to local consensus instead of stopping;
- the same cooldown mechanism handles hierarchical partitions.

## Limitations

Both groups are still hosted on one machine. The experiment validates the
topology and failure semantics, not real WAN RTT or packet loss. A future
round should run the bridge and Group B on a physically separate host.
