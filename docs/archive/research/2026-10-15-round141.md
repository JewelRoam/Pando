# Research Round 141 — A third wording set, and what the text is worth

Date: 2026-10-15

## The instrument the last three rounds kept asking for

Rounds 129, 137 and 140 each ended at the same wall: the fine structure they
were measuring was the same size as the sampling error. And the first attempt
at this round hit a subtler version of it - a text model trained on wording
set B looked *in-sample on dev* as well, because dev and the old test split
share their phrasings (round 122's leak), so a combiner fitted on dev would
have learned to trust a feature that is not that good in the field.

`tools/build_large_suite.py` now carries a **third inventory** (`--cues c`, 48
phrasings) and `assets/heldout2_test.jsonl` is the 240-row suite built from
it. Verified against both earlier inventories: **0 of 48 cues overlap**, so
three disjoint wording sets now exist and the two roles can be separated:

| set | used for |
|---|---|
| A (dev, seed 1) | fitting the combiner |
| B (heldout, seed 3) | training the text model |
| **C (heldout2, seed 4)** | **evaluation - unseen by both** |

The neural trio was then run over C: majority 77.08% against the best single
member's 73.75%, and the gate accepts 163 of 240 rows at 90.80%.

## The measurement

Everything is fitted on A (votes) and B (text), and scored once on C:

| model | full coverage | the gate's 163 rows | the 77 declined rows |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| best single member | 73.75% | 89.57% | 40.26% |
| shipped gate | 77.08% | 90.80% | - (it declines) |
| combiner, votes only | 80.83% | 91.41% | 58.44% |
| **combiner + text posterior** | **82.92%** | **92.64%** | **62.34%** |
| oracle (answer in the trio) | 85.83% | 92.64% | 71.43% |

(the oracle figures were recomputed independently of the tool before being
believed: 206/240 overall, 151/163 on the accepted rows - the combined model
matches it there exactly, which is a coincidence of this split and not a bug)

## What it says

**The swarm now clearly beats its members, on wording none of it has seen.**
82.92% against the best single member's 73.75% is +9.2 points, and the gap
that rounds 118-120 could only describe is now mostly claimed: on the 77 rows
the hand-written gate declines, the combined model answers **62.34%** against
the best member's 40.26% and an oracle of 71.43% - about three-quarters of
what was available, and it reaches that with a rule that is 33 numbers wide
per class.

**The vote pattern carries most of the signal; the text adds a real but
smaller increment.** Votes alone reach 58.44% on the declined rows, the text
posterior lifts that to 62.34% (+3.9 points, +2.1 overall). That ordering is
itself informative: the members' disagreement pattern is the strongest
available evidence about a hard row, and the text model - trained on *other
wording* - is a secondary signal, which is what round 137 predicted when it
found the cheap members' individual value unpredictable but their
independence real.

**And the swarm's own gain is visible on this split too**: the plain majority
beats the best member by 3.3 points here, where split B showed 1.1. The
ensemble effect is real; it was just small next to the combiner's.

## What this changes

The shipped artifact (`assets/combiner.json`, spec v1, vote patterns only)
should become spec v2 with the text posterior appended, and the trainer needs
one more rule: **the text model and the combiner must be fitted on wording
disjoint from each other and from the evaluation**, which the three sets now
make possible. That is the next round's work, and it is now a deployment
detail rather than a question.
