  member stronger than the one it was meant to supplement, at least on one

  incumbent and F 3.3; the capability floor is incumbent + 4 on unseen

  recipe sample is a draw; ±4-5 points here is inside the measured spread.

So the disciplined next step is the one the project already knows how to
take: **replicate the recipe** (2 epochs, mixed corpus) across seeds and
judge by the sample mean before any generation replaces a seated member.
Seed 6 is already training (prediction recorded: D within 2 points of
0.9792; the recipe's mean, not its best draw, is the admission argument).

For deployment maths in the meantime: the registry's mean-accuracy rule
still seats ft-v3 (its D/E mean 0.9667 beats ft-v7's 0.9646) - the system
prefers the more even member, which is what a mean is for.
