## What E moved

**The assent tier reproduces stronger than on D**: 227 auto-executed rows,
every one correct (D: 195). On a fresh inventory the gate's
precision-at-coverage is the part of the system that got *better*, not
worse - the anti-overfitting property the tiered design promised.

**The demoted rows' answer is a policy slot, not a fact.** On D the advisor
resolves the ft-v3-dissent rows better (0.9833 vs override 0.9417); on E the
trusted member resolves them better (0.9917 vs advisor 0.9583). Over both
splits the two fallbacks are near-tied (0.9708 vs 0.9667 across 480 rows).
The wrong lesson would be to pick one; the right one is that the fallback
must be a declared, per-deployment validated choice - Task 008 (dispatched)
makes it `--assent-fallback advisor|veto` with tests.

**Honest edges.** The advisor's collapse on E's 13 disputed rows (3 correct)
is itself worth noting: the combiner's text features are out of distribution
on E wording, so on E "advisor" is not the same instrument it was on D. The
veto fallback has not yet been exercised live; that is the next run once
Task 008 lands. And `mac-llm` still carries constant p=0.5 under the B
prompt.

### Update — the fallback slot lands, and the veto runs live

Task 008 delivered `--assent-fallback advisor|veto` (Coordinator param,
CLI, `/status`, veto field carrying the trusted member's acting choice;
agreement/mean_probability stay the aggregate's). Extracted to main
file-by-file (rootd) plus the commit's own test diff; smoke 23/23.

Live on E with the veto fallback: **auto 227 @ 1.0000, decided 0.9750**
(advisor fallback live: 0.9583; offline predicted 0.9917). The 4-row gap
against the offline override is the demoted set drifting on float margins
between recorded votes and the live run - the direction (veto > advisor on
E) holds on both, and the opposite held on D offline. The honest summary:
the slot is real, the choice is deployment-validated, and the live
accounting for veto rows is now explicit in the checker (a veto row's
acting answer is `choice`, not `suggested_choice` - the first live run of
the day measured the advisory field by mistake and showed 0.9583).

### Update 2 — the assent seat picks itself (Task 009)

Task 009 (Mac) landed `--assent-checkpoint auto`: a checked-in
`assets/registry/accuracies.json` (per-member measured accuracy with
source-file provenance), resolved at decision time among the present
(collapsed) voters by highest mean measured accuracy; unknown checkpoints
are ignored, empty registries fall back to no assent with one warning, and
the resolution is reported (`assent` in the response, `assent_resolved` in
`/status`). Merging it with the fallback branch needed a real three-way
merge (Task 009 was based on the pre-fallback commit) - conflicts resolved
by hand, 24/24 smoke green on Windows.

Live on E with `--assent-checkpoint auto --assent-fallback veto`: the
registry resolved to `ft-v3` (highest mean 0.967 across D/E) and the run
produced **auto 227 @ 1.0000, decided 0.9750** - identical to the explicit
`ft-v3` run. The last hard-coded name in the gating policy is gone.
