# Research Round 23 — suite expanded to 40 tasks; score anomaly found (2026-10-14)

- Suite expanded 16 -> 40 (deterministic-label templates; 24 new: en 8,
  ml 8 across 8 languages, td 8 workflow states). Generator unpacking bug
  found and fixed (candidates/expected transposed); suite repaired.
- Results (CPU, per lineage): english 0.714 acc / ECE 0.155 / 52.4 ms;
  multilingual 0.786 / 0.144 / 24.7 ms; typed-decisions 0.583 / 0.213 /
  58.3 ms.
- **Score anomaly**: score-type tasks fail across ALL lineages (en 1/3,
  ml 0/3, td 1/3) while choice/noul are strong (ml: 11/11). Score answers
  are float expected-values parsed against level descriptions - either the
  models are genuinely weak at level ranking or the level-description
  semantics misalign with the regression head. Flagged for investigation
  before any score-type gating is trusted.
- Calibration improved for english (0.36 -> 0.15) with the more balanced
  task mix; multilingual remains best overall (acc 0.79, ECE 0.14, 24.7ms).
- Caveats: synthetic distribution; 40 tasks is still small-sample; the
  score anomaly dominates the error budget.
