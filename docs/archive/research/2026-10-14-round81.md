# Research Round 81 — Post-training temperature calibration

Date: 2026-10-14

## Calibration setup

`tools/calibrate_checkpoint.py` fits a temperature map on the labeled
training suite while keeping the six-task holdout untouched.

For v7:

```text
calibration items: 22 choice items
fitted temperature: [2.6704, 1.0, 1.0]
buckets: choice:3-5 = 21, choice:2 = 1
```

The choice temperature was sharpened/softened from 1.0 to about 2.67,
which reduces confidence rather than increasing it.

## Holdout confidence

| task | v7 before | v7 calibrated |
|---|---:|---:|
| en01 correct refund | 1.0000 | 0.9819 |
| ml04 wrong risk | 0.3950 | 0.2836 |
| td04 wrong action | 0.5589 | 0.4659 |
| ml12b wrong refund | **0.9996** | **0.9314** |
| td06 correct route | 0.9263 | 0.7207 |

Mean holdout confidence fell from 0.7760 to 0.6767. The worst wrong answer
moved away from saturation, but did not become correct.

Holdout accuracy stayed at 0.4 on the five choice tasks. ECE moved from
0.3760 to 0.3884, so this five-task slice did not supply evidence of better
calibration.

## Live A/B after calibration

The calibrated checkpoint was exported to ONNX and placed against the
baseline in the real root:

```text
decision agreement: 6/6
baseline accuracy:  0.5
candidate accuracy: 0.5
wrong ml12b confidence: 0.9316
```

It still did not promote: no label correction and no held-out calibration
win. Its behavior is less overconfident than uncalibrated v7, but the
evidence is not strong enough to replace the baseline.

## Interpretation

The calibration machinery is now operational and reproducible:

```text
labeled suite
  -> calibration_records()
  -> fit_temperature_map()
  -> calibrated checkpoint
  -> ONNX export
  -> live A/B
```

The remaining blocker is data quality and size, not implementation.
