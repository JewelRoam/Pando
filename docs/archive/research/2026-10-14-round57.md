## Quorum analysis

- unanimous: 26/40 (65%)
- split (disagreement): **14/40 (35%)**
- → **14 training-ready contrast pairs** already available for RLCD

## Key findings

1. **en vs td is the most similar pair** (90% agreement) - the typed-decisions
   fine-tune closely tracks the english base on most questions.
2. **en vs ml is the most divergent pair** (32.5% divergence) - the
   multilingual model genuinely sees text differently, providing the most
   independent evidence.
3. **35% of tasks produce split votes** - this is the sweet spot: enough
   disagreement to prove the lineages are independent, not so much that
   the swarm is unreliable. The 14 contrast pairs are the RLCD fuel.
4. The divergence rate validates the checkpoint-diverse quorum requirement:
   with homogeneous replicas, divergence would be 0% and there would be
   zero training signal.
