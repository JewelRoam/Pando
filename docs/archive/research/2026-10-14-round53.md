## IsNaN (30x) is the new leading suspect
ModernBERT uses IsNaN for attention mask sanitization. The vpux compiler
likely cannot handle 30 NaN-check nodes. Fix: preprocess the ONNX graph to
replace IsNaN+Where pairs with Mul (multiply by 0/1 mask) - a standard
optimization for inference-only graphs.
