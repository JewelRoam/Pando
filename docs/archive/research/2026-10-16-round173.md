# Research Round 173 — The fresh judge speaks: seed variance, and a committee that clears it

Date: 2026-10-16

Split H (240 rows, 48 cues none of the loop has ever touched) is the first
evaluator that no generation, teacher, or selection step has seen. The
whole ladder ran through it:

| member | H (fresh judge) |
|---|---:|
| english (floor) | 0.8333 |
| ft-v3 (incumbent) | 0.9500 |
| ft-v7 (seed 5) | **0.9958** |
| ft-v8 (seed 6) | 0.9458 |
| mac-llm-8b (the teacher) | 0.9333 |
| mac-llm (4B, co-teacher) | 0.9208 |

Two results that matter, and one that reframes the recipe:

**A machine-trained member beats its teacher and the incumbent on a fresh
judge** - ft-v7 at 0.9958 (+4.6 over the incumbent, +6.2 over the 8B that
labeled its corpus). The loop can now *produce* capability, not just
redistribute it.

**And the seed axis reappears where it counts.** ft-v8, the same recipe,
lands at 0.9458 - five points below its sibling and a hair under the
incumbent. On D/E/F the two seeds agreed within 1.25 points; on the fresh
judge they part company. The recipe's *mean* (0.9708) clears the incumbent
by only +2.1, under the +4 capability floor - so the admission rule refuses
again, and correctly: one of these two draws would have been an
unrepeatable win.

**The committee construction clears both problems.** The two seeds agree
on 94.2% of H - and on every agreed row they are right (226/226). The 14
disagreements go to the incumbent as tiebreak, who wins 13. Result: **four
of five committee outputs correct at full coverage - 0.9958**, matching the
best single seed while no longer depending on being that seed. That is the
PandoSI thesis in its smallest form: not one lucky student, but a seated
incumbent arbitrating a young generation's disagreements.

Next steps this opens (in order): more seeds to price the committee's
variance honestly; a formal "committee member" that votes as one; and a
stronger teacher (Task 025 dispatched: deploy a higher-precision 8B or a
14B-class tier and run it through H before any new corpus is commissioned).
