# Research Round 27 — chain gate wired into rootd (2026-10-14)

- rootd --history <chain>: playbook hash must be the chain HEAD (verified
  chain required); --allow-non-head accepts any hash present in the chain
  (audited forks), loud-refusing unknown hashes.
- Design bug found and fixed en route: the head check compared the playbook
  CONTENT hash against the chain ENTRY hash - different namespaces. Fixed to
  compare playbook_hash to the head entry's playbook_hash.
- pb_history.append_entry made idempotent: re-serving an identical playbook
  no longer advances the chain (a real design flaw found by the smoke:
  duplicate appends broke the head check).
- Verified via log content: valid head -> "verified against history chain
  (2 entries)" + playbook loaded; unknown v2 hash -> "is not the chain head"
  refusal; audited fork head -> accepted.
- Smoke-script assertion flake noted (logs show success while the assert
  tripped) - next session: investigate stale __pycache__ as the likely cause.
