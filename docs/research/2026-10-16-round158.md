# Research Round 158 — The first real LLM tier: deployed, evaluated, and honestly not yet a win

Date: 2026-10-16

## What was deployed (by the Mac node, via the task mailbox)

- Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507, Q4_K_M (~2.33 GiB), llama.cpp 0.6.0 (build 11429)
  with Apple Metal (-ngl 99), OpenAI-compatible on `:8080`, API-key guarded

## Reading it honestly

**Zero-shot 4B is not smarter than the tuned small model on this task family.**
Qwen3-4B lands at 0.7375 - within a point of untuned Laya english (0.7292) -
while the 3070's fine-tuned Laya decides 0.9417. "Bigger model" alone buys
nothing here; the self-trained member is still the best single tier.

**The LLM tier's confidence is a poison and a lever at once.** Its mean
reported probability is 0.9586 against 0.7375 accuracy (ECE 0.2211, every
vote in the top bin). Seating it swings the gate wide open: auto coverage
159 -> 225 rows. But the system's decided accuracy *falls* 0.9750 -> 0.9167:
the gate is being bought by a foreign, uncalibrated confidence scale. The
combination layer - not the model - is now the bottleneck, exactly the
falsification branch round 157 named in advance.

**A third complaint is a real signal.** With mac-llm seated, 225 rows
auto-execute at 0.9244. If calibration keeps the coverage and recovers the
accuracy, the three-tier fleet beats the pair; if it cannot, tiered
composition needs a confidence-policy per tier, not just per member.
