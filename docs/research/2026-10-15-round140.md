# Research Round 140 — Ship the combiner, and keep it advisory

Date: 2026-10-15

## From measurement to artifact

Round 139 measured that a fitted combiner over the quorum's votes and
calibrated confidences beats every single member (80.4% against 75.4% on 240
wording-held-out rows) and is 54.4% accurate on the rows the hand-written gate
*declines*, where the best member reaches 41.2% and the oracle is 67.7%. This
round makes it a file the swarm can load and an answer the swarm can attach.

Three pieces:

- `pandosi/combiner.py` - the feature spec, the model, and the artifact
  format. Features are addressed by **lineage**, not by node, because a
  quorum's members depend on which stems were picked while the admitted
  lineages are stable; an absent lineage contributes zeros.
- `tools/train_combiner.py` - fits from recorded reports, chooses the
  regularisation by cross-validation on the training split alone, and writes
  `assets/combiner.json`: **4 KB**, the same order of magnitude as a
  calibration table, which is the point - it is an artifact a playbook can
  sign and a device can hold.
- `pandosi/rootd.py` - `--combiner <path>`, and a `suggested_choice` /
  `suggested_probability` pair attached to the response **only when the gate
  does not auto-execute**. Nothing about a tier, a choice or an
  auto-executable path changes.

## Verified, on rows it has never seen

`tools/combiner_artifact_check.py` replays the held-out report through the
shipped file:

| | full coverage | on the 68 declined rows |
|---|---:|---:|
| shipped gate | 77.08% | - (it declines) |
| best single member | 75.42% | 41.18% |
| **artifact** | **80.42%** | **54.41%** |

suggestions offered on 68 rows, **0 of them at tier `auto`** - the advisory
property holds, which is the property that makes shipping it safe.

## A defect the live check caught

Wiring it into a running root immediately produced a nonsense suggestion: the
model, fitted on one question schema's eight labels, answered `complaint` to a
question whose candidates were `approve / reject / hold`. A member's vote is
restricted to the request's candidates; a combiner's answer has to be too.

`suggest` now takes the candidate list and returns nothing when the model's
labels do not intersect it, and the root passes `req.candidates`. Re-checked
live: with foreign candidates there is no suggestion, with the schema's own
candidates the suggestion appears and the tier stays where the gate put it.

That is the same contract the project has enforced since round 102, applied to
a new kind of voter - and it was found by running it rather than by reading
it, which is the argument for keeping one live check even when the offline
replay looks clean.

## Where the swarm stands

The decision path now has three layers with measured roles:

1. **the members** - decorrelated voices, admitted on independence (rounds
   122-137);
2. **the gate** - hand-written thresholds that are 0.6 points from the oracle
   on the rows they accept, and that own safety (rounds 118-136);
3. **the fitted combiner** - advisory, answering where the gate declines,
   worth about three-quarters of the headroom that is left (rounds 139-140).
