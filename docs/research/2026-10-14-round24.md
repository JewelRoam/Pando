# Research Round 24 — score anomaly investigation closed (2026-10-14)

## Verdict: score heads are not decision-grade on any lineage

Raw-output investigation of all 9 score tasks:

1. english + multilingual: score outputs are CONTENT-BLIND. All three tasks
   per lineage produce ~1.2-1.7 regardless of content ("smoke from charging
   cable" = "opening hours question"). The score head emits a near-constant
   prior. Genuinely unusable.
2. typed-decisions: the ONLY content-sensitive score head (risk ranking is
   correct in ORDER: login-stuffing 3.19 > ticket 3.06 > email prefs 1.48).
   But the bucket distributions are flat/bimodal between adjacent high
   levels (td02: top bucket critical 0.415 vs high 0.348; expected high).
   Rounding-parse passes td02; argmax-parse flips it - both parses disagree,
   which itself proves the output is not decision-grade.

## Policy adopted

- score-type questions are demoted to INFORMATIONAL in the playbook format:
  they may be displayed/logged but MUST NOT gate auto-execution.
- Gate-worthy risk judgements should be expressed as choice questions with
  explicit criteria (where lineages score 5/6, 6/6, 4/6) or routed to the
  typed-decisions lineage with a distribution-spread check.
- graft_eval keeps both parses (round + argmax) in its details for future
  lineages.

## Suite caveat

The 40-task suite's error budget is dominated by this single systematic
issue; excluding score tasks, lineage accuracies rise to en 5/6+4/5=0.90*,
ml 11/11=1.00*, td 6/9=0.67 (*small samples).
