# Research Round 142 — The artifact grows a text model

Date: 2026-10-15

## What round 141 asked for

Round 141 separated the three wording sets and measured, on the one split
neither model had seen, that a combiner over the members' votes plus a text
model's posterior reaches **82.92%** at full coverage (best single member
73.75%, shipped gate 77.08%) and **62.34%** on the rows the gate declines
(best member 40.26%, oracle 71.43%). It ended with a deployment note rather
than a question: the artifact is spec v1 and sees only votes, and the text
model and the combiner have to be fitted on disjoint wording.

## The change

`pandosi/combiner.py` is now **spec v2**: the feature block appends the text
model's posterior over the candidates, and the artifact **embeds the text
model** rather than referencing it, so one signed file carries both
(**35 KB** - still the order of a calibration table). Features are 33 wide:
25 describing the vote pattern, 8 the text posterior.

`tools/train_combiner.py` gains the two arguments the deployment rule needs:
`--reports` (the votes the combiner fits on), `--suite` (the tasks those rows
came from, for the text) and `--text-train` (the wording the text model learns
from), and it records both provenances in the artifact's metadata. The rule is
now enforced by habit and documented in the flag's help text: **a text model
fitted on the combiner's own rows looks near-perfect while fitting and is not**
- the leak this round's predecessor hit at 99.2% cross-validation.

## Verified on the only clean split

`tools/combiner_artifact_check.py` replays split C - unseen by the combiner
(fitted on A) and by the embedded text model (fitted on B):

| | full coverage | the gate's 163 rows | the 77 declined rows |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| shipped gate | 77.08% | 90.80% | - |
| best single member | 73.75% | 89.57% | 40.26% |
| **artifact (spec 2)** | **82.92%** | 92.64% | **62.34%** |

Suggestions offered on 77 rows, **0 of them at tier `auto`**: the advisory
property from round 140 survives the new feature block.

## And in a running root

A root with `--combiner` loads the spec-2 file, and its responses carry
`suggested_choice` only where the tier is not `auto`. A mock-fleet smoke
confirmed the text features reach the model at runtime - two requests whose
votes were identical but whose text differed produced different suggestion
probabilities (0.47 against 0.58) - which is what "the text is in the
artifact" has to mean in practice. (The mock's *choices* are artificial by
construction; the quality claim rests on the split-C replay above.)

## Where the decision path stands

| layer | role | evidence |
|---|---|---|
| members | decorrelated voices, admitted on independence | rounds 122-137 |
| gate | safety and auto-execution; 0.6 points from the oracle on what it accepts | rounds 118-136 |
| combiner (spec 2) | advisory; decides what the gate declines | rounds 139-142, evaluated on split C |

The chain from "the ensemble might not beat its best member" (round 117) now
reads: the swarm beats its best member by 3.3 points on its own (split C), and
a fitted advisory combiner takes the same rows to 9.2 points over the best
member - with the gate's safety untouched and the artifact small enough to
ship with a playbook.
