# Research Round 26 — playbook version history chain (2026-10-14)

- `pandosi/pb_history.py`: append-only hash chain for playbook versions.
  Entry = {seq, parent, playbook_hash, ts, hash}; hash = sha256 of
  parent|playbook_hash|ts|seq. verify_chain recomputes everything.
- Fork model: each lineage is its own chain file; a fork's genesis parent is
  the source entry hash (verify_chain takes genesis_parent). Wrong-genesis
  verification correctly fails.
- Smoke-verified: main lineage chain ok; fork chain with correct genesis ok;
  wrong genesis detected; content tampering detected with precise error.
- Connects to Phase 5: RLCD-produced checkpoints/playbooks append to the
  chain as new entries; the chain IS the auditable evolution history.
- Next: rootd --playbook should refuse a playbook whose hash is not the
  chain head (or an explicitly whitelisted fork head).
