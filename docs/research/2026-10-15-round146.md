# Research Round 146 — Growing and shrinking a fleet while it serves

Date: 2026-10-15

## The integration nobody had run

The growth machinery was built in rounds 109-116 - adaptive quorum, retirement,
rollback - before the rules that decide what a member *means* existed: a
replica is one opinion (121), a correlated family is one opinion (129), the
cheapest member serves (136), and the governor declares all of it (145). Every
one of those rules was measured on its own; no fleet had been grown and shrunk
while it served.

`tools/elastic_fleet_check.py` runs four stages against one live root, driving
the same 60 labelled rows through each, over real HTTP:

| stage | decisions/s | p50 | opinions | auto-executed | accuracy there | decided |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| one leaf (iGPU) | 21.25 | 44 ms | **1** | 50 | 0.800 | 0.717 |
| **+ a new opinion** (the 19 KB text member) | 20.71 | 46 ms | **2** | 15 | **1.000** | 0.733 |
| **+ a replica** (english again, on the NPU) | 20.61 | 46 ms | **2** | 15 | 1.000 | 0.733 |
| **− the replica** | 20.50 | 46 ms | 2 | 15 | 1.000 | 0.733 |

## What each kind of growth buys

**A new opinion buys trustworthy automation.** The fleet moves from agreing
with itself - one member, agreement 1.0, 50 of 60 rows auto-executed at 80%
- to a two-opinion quorum that auto-executes only a quarter of the rows but is
right on **every one it does** (15/15 on this sample; the honest reading is
"1.000 on fifteen rows", not a claim of perfection). Decided accuracy, which
counts the combiner's suggestions on the rows the gate declines, rises from
0.717 to 0.733.

**A replica buys nothing measurable.** Adding `english` a second time - on
different silicon, the NPU - leaves the quorum at **two opinions**, leaves
throughput where it was, and leaves the answers identical. That is round 121's
rule and rounds 133/138's economics showing up live: the second copy is the
same voice, and the fleet can tell.

**Removal is free.** Retiring the replica changes nothing at all - not the
opinion count, not the throughput, not an answer. A member that contributed no
opinion can leave without the fleet noticing, which is what a retirement
policy needs to be safe by construction.

## What this closes

The elastic premise - more devices, same mind - now has a live demonstration on
both of its axes:

| axis | what growth does | evidence |
|---|---|---|
| capacity | adds units, capped by the host | rounds 132-133 |
| **quality** | adds *opinions*; a replica is not one | this round, live |
| trust | more opinions means fewer auto-executions, more accurate ones | this round, live |

and the shrinkage side is symmetric: a member whose opinion the fleet already
holds can be removed for free.
